Frequently asked questions

What is camelCase?

camelCase joins words without spaces and capitalizes every word after the first, e.g. caseConverter.

How does Title Case work?

It capitalizes the first letter of each word, which is the convention used for headings and titles.

Is conversion done locally?

驼峰命名去掉空格连接单词，并大写首个单词之后的每个词，例如 caseConverter。
