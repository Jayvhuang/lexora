Frequently asked questions

How is reading time calculated?

We assume an average adult reads about 200 words per minute (WPM); reading time = words ÷ 200.

Does the counter include spaces?

Yes — by default we show characters both with and without spaces so you can choose the metric your platform uses.

Is my text uploaded to a server?

No. All counting happens in your browser; nothing is sent or stored.

常见问题

阅读时间是如何计算的？

我们假设普通成年人每分钟约阅读 200 个单词（WPM）；阅读时间 = 单词数 ÷ 200。

计数是否包含空格？

包含——默认同时显示含空格与不含空格的字符数，方便你选择对应平台所需的指标。

我的文本会被上传到服务器吗？

不会。所有统计都在浏览器中完成，内容不会被发送或存储。
