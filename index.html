Frequently asked questions

Is my text uploaded to a server?

No. Every tool runs entirely in your browser. Nothing you type or paste is sent to or stored on any server.

Do I need to create an account?

No account, no sign-up, no installation. Open Lexora and start using the tools immediately.

What tools are included?

A word & character counter, case converter, text cleaner, lorem ipsum generator, text diff, find & replace, random picker and readability scorer.

Is Lexora free?

Yes — all tools are free to use. The site may show contextual ads once an ad account is connected, but the tools themselves remain free.
