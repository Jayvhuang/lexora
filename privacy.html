Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 21, 2026

1. Overview

Lexora provides free, browser-based text and writing tools. This policy explains what information is collected when you visit our website and how it is used. Our core tools run entirely in your browser, so the text you type or paste is never uploaded to or stored on our servers.

2. Information we collect automatically

When you visit Lexora, our hosting provider and any advertising partner may automatically collect certain technical information, including:

• IP address — used for security, analytics, and to serve region-appropriate advertising.
• Cookies and similar technologies — including third-party cookies set by advertising partners such as Google AdSense.
• Web beacons / pixel tags — small invisible images that help measure advertising impressions and performance.

3. Third-party advertising (Google AdSense)

Lexora may display advertisements served by Google AdSense. Google and its partners may use cookies, web beacons, and your IP address to personalize ads and measure their performance. These are third-party cookies set and read by Google, not by Lexora.

Google's use of advertising cookies is governed by the Google Ads Settings and its partner policies. You can learn how Google uses data when you use our site and our partners' sites here: How Google uses data when you use our partners' sites or apps.

4. Amazon Associates

Lexora is a participant in the Amazon Associates program. As an Amazon Associate, Lexora earns from qualifying purchases. Links to Amazon are tagged with our associate ID and may use cookies to track referrals.

5. Your choices

You can disable or clear cookies through your browser settings at any time. Disabling cookies may affect how advertising is personalized but will not break the tools.

6. Contact

For privacy questions, contact: privacy@lexora.com

隐私政策

最后更新：2026年7月21日

1. 概览

Lexora 提供免费的浏览器端文本与写作工具。本政策说明您访问本网站时被收集的信息及其用途。我们的核心工具完全在您的浏览器中运行，因此您输入或粘贴的文本绝不会被上传或存储到我们的服务器。

2. 我们自动收集的信息

当您访问 Lexora 时，我们的托管服务商及广告合作伙伴可能会自动收集以下技术信息：

• IP 地址——用于安全防护、分析与投放符合地区特征的广告。
• Cookie 及类似技术——包括由 Google AdSense 等广告合作伙伴设置的第三方 Cookie。
• 网络信标 / 像素标签（web beacon）——用于衡量广告展示量与效果的小型不可见图像。

3. 第三方广告（Google AdSense）

Lexora 可能展示由 Google AdSense 投放的广告。Google 及其合作伙伴可能使用 Cookie、网络信标以及您的IP 地址来个性化广告并衡量其效果。这些是由 Google（而非 Lexora）设置与读取的第三方 Cookie。

Google 对广告 Cookie 的使用受其广告设置与合作伙伴政策约束。您可在此了解 Google 如何使用您的数据：Google 如何使用数据（当您使用我们的合作伙伴网站或应用时）。

4. Amazon Associates

Lexora 是 Amazon Associates 计划的参与者。作为 Amazon Associate，Lexora 从符合条件的购买中获得收益。指向 Amazon 的链接带有我们的联盟 ID，并可能使用 Cookie 追踪推荐。

5. 您的选择

您可以随时通过浏览器设置禁用或清除 Cookie。禁用 Cookie 可能影响广告的个性化程度，但不会导致工具不可用。

6. 联系方式

隐私相关问题请联系：privacy@lexora.com
